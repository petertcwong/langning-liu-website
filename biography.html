"Whoever says Lang-Ning Liu must also say Bach."

-Leipzig Bach Archive

Hailed by Anthony Tommasini of The New York Times as "a sensitive, extremely compelling artist," acclaimed virtuoso Lang-Ning Liu brings an introspective approach to both the contemporary and classical repertoire, with performances that couple breathtaking technical mastery with noble musicianship. Her uncommon artistic range and the compelling immediacy of her interpretations offer listeners distinctively original concert experiences.

Lang-Ning Liu is an internationally acclaimed virtuoso who has performed with leading orchestras including the Beijing Symphony, Juilliard Symphony, and Prague Radio Symphony. Her artistic scope spans debuts at major venues worldwide and landmark premieres such as Charles Ives' Emerson Concerto with The Juilliard Orchestra. After winning several major prizes and prestigious awards, Ms. Liu's career has taken her throughout North America, Europe, and Asia. She made her performance debut and debut CD recording with the Prague Radio Symphony Orchestra—released following a Canadian tour with the Toronto Concert Orchestra under the late maestro Kerry Stratton's baton, to whom the album is dedicated. Critics raved of her "spellbinding and dramatic like-lightning" interpretation of Tchaikovsky's Piano Concerto No. 1, while the Barrie Examiner wrote: "From the first crashing chords, juxtaposed with the famous opening theme in the brass, to passages of quiet lyricism and extroverted energy, Liu's playing was both expressive and fittingly spectacular. The enthused crowd rose to their feet in appreciation of a truly inspirational evening." Highlight performance engagements include soloist appearances with the New Jersey Symphony Orchestra, Juarez Symphony Orchestra, Southern Missouri Symphony, Louisiana Philharmonic, Slovak State Philharmonic Orchestra, Liaoning Symphony Orchestra, Toronto Concert Orchestra, Cathedral Bluffs Symphony, University of Toronto Wind Ensemble, and Chinese Artists Society of Toronto Orchestra.

Ms. Liu is a recording artist for several editions of Frederick Harris Music's acclaimed Celebration Series Perspectives in association with The Royal Conservatory of Music. She has made media appearances on radio, TV, and in print on Canada's largest broadcast and publishing networks, as well as those abroad. Notable media appearances include Czech Television, CCTV China, CBC Music, CTV's Bravo! & CP24 TV, The WholeNote, Fairchild TV, Rogers TV, OMNI TV, InsideToronto, Sing Tao Daily, M2TV, and numerous guest appearances on CFMZ-FM Classical 96.3. A passionate interpreter of new music, she premiered Charles Ives' Emerson Concerto with The Juilliard Symphony Orchestra and conductor Anne Manson in New York—a performance that brought the cheering audience to its feet—and has premiered and recorded commissioned works by composers including Juno Award-winner Vincent Ho and the late Clifford Crawley.

In addition to her concertizing activities, Ms. Liu devotes herself to educational and artistic outreach. A dedicated pedagogue, she serves on faculty at The Glenn Gould School and The Taylor Academy for Young Artists at The Royal Conservatory of Music, and founded the Toronto International Piano Competition. In 2013, she was appointed to the Board of Directors at the Chinese Cultural Centre of Greater Toronto (CCC) as Advisor of Culture & Education. She has been an Advisory Board Member of the International Resource Centre for Performing Artists since 2011 and is a frequent recipient of artistic grants recognizing her contributions across Canada, the United States, and Asia.

Ms. Liu enjoys a reputation as a highly sought-after teacher whose students consistently win top prizes at major international competitions—including the Gina Bachauer, Canadian Chopin, Hilton Head, Cleveland, and Quebec Music Competitions—and are frequently accepted with full scholarships to elite institutions worldwide. Her students hold admissions to The Juilliard School, Curtis Institute of Music, Royal Academy of Music, Guildhall School of Music & Drama, New England Conservatory, Oberlin College, Mannes School of Music, Eastman School of Music, The Tianjin Juilliard School, McGill University, Peabody Institute, The Glenn Gould School, and The Taylor Academy. She regularly gives master classes across North America and Asia and serves as adjudicator for numerous competitions. She is also a featured artist-educator at the International Music Exchange Festival in Shenyang, China, preparing students for performance.

A native of China, Ms. Liu began piano studies at age six and made her debut in a local auditorium at eight. At eleven, she entered Beijing's Central Conservatory of Music with highest honours, making her orchestral debut with the Beijing Symphony Orchestra in Beethoven's Piano Concerto No. 1. She continued her training at The Glenn Gould School in Toronto on full scholarship from the Sing Tao Daily Foundation, studying with Marietta Orlov. She completed her Master's at The Juilliard School under Julian Martin, with additional studies alongside Vladimir Ashkenazy, James Anagnoson, and Dang Thai Son—all of whom profoundly shaped her artistry.
