Lang-Ning Liu

Concert Pianist | Educator

"… As realized, the work is more than 20 minutes of turbulent, stream-of-consciousness Ives, and the soloist, Lang-Ning Liu, rightly received a rousing ovation for her incisive, fearless and vividly colored account of the daunting piano part."

Anthony Tommasini, The New York Times

Lang-Ning Liu is an internationally acclaimed virtuoso with leading orchestras including the Beijing Symphony, Juilliard Symphony, and the Prague Radio Symphony orchestras. Her artistic scope spans debuts at major venues worldwide and spans landmark premieres such as Charles Ives' Emerson Concerto with The Juilliard Orchestra - and acclaimed educational recordings for the Royal Conservatory's Celebration Series Perspectives.

A dedicated pedagogue, she serves on faculty at The Glenn Gould School and The Taylor Academy, and founded the Toronto International Piano Competition. She mentors students who have consistently win top prizes at major competitions and accepted to elite institutions worldwide including Juilliard, Curtis, Royal Academy of Music, Guildhall, and New England conservatory.

Reminiscence

In 2015, Lang-Ning Liu's performance of the Tchaikovsky Piano Concerto No. 1 with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra became the stuff of legend. The London Evening Standard described it as "a performance that transcended technique to reveal the very soul of the music." Her interpretation, characterized by both power and poetry, captivated the audience from the first note, culminating in a standing ovation that lasted over 10 minutes.

"...The keyboard part, an Olympic challenge, was hurled forth with miraculous strength and confidence breadth by Lang-Ning Liu, whose delicate appearance, it quickly turned out, could not have been more misleading."

Shirley Fleming, Musical America

Education & Outreach

Lang Ning Liu approaches education and artistic outreach with the same dedication and artistry that defines her performances. She believes that music's transformative power transcends borders and backgrounds, and she is committed to sharing this gift through teaching, mentorship, and creative programming that connects audiences with the profound emotional and intellectual depth of the piano repertoire.

Faculty Member
The Glenn Gould School

Teaching piano performance at one of Canada's most prestigious music institutions, mentoring advanced students for professional careers and international competitions.

Faculty Member
The Taylor Academy for Young Artists

Mentoring exceptionally talented young pianists in one of North America's premier pre-college training programs.

Founder and Artistic Director
Toronto International Piano Competition

Established and leads one of Canada's premier piano competitions, creating opportunities for emerging artists to perform on international stages and develop their professional careers.

Board Member
Chinese Cultural Centre of Greater Toronto
Advisor of Culture & Education

Advising on cultural and educational initiatives that bridge communities through music and the arts.

Lang Ning Liu's teaching philosophy emphasizes artistic integrity, technical excellence, and personal expression. She draws from her own experiences performing on the world's most prestigious stages to guide her students toward developing their unique artistic voices while mastering the craft of piano performance. Her commitment to education extends beyond the studio through masterclasses, jury service at international competitions, and her leadership in developing innovative programming that brings classical music to new audiences.

"The soloist, a highly gifted and promising pianist ... She brought down the house with an exceptional, poetic account of Chopin's Piano Concerto, the great E - Concerto, Op. 11 ... She brought to it a huge dynamic range and she characterized each of its three movements with an arresting sense of the movement's individuality, its specific gravity and its music. The entire work fell naturally into place in Liu's coherent reading."

The Whole Note Magazine
